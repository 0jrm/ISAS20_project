1. Mission

This campaign asks whether NeSPReSO temperature and salinity, written into TSIS as profiles, can improve a Gulf of Mexico HYCOM forecast after the model has run four days away from a reanalysis restart, and it scores that answer against a traditional Argo-plus-satellite analysis on the same drifted background.

Start from the 1 Jan 2024 18Z reanalysis restart. Integrate four days with no assimilation. At 6 Jan 00Z, run TSIS once for each DA member against that drifted state. Apply the increment over the next 24 hours and forecast through 12 Jan 18Z. Four twins share the restart. null gets no increment. ops is cargo Argo plus L3/L4 SLA plus L4 SST, the operational-style benchmark. nespreso_argo is nine NeSPReSO columns at the 6 Jan GDAC sites. nespreso_tracks is NeSPReSO sampled on a 0.25° thinning of cargo L3 SLA along-track points from 4 Jan and 5 Jan, the two days before analysis. Score all four against GDAC Argo on each calendar day. The older sibling da_loop_20240102 assimilated six hours after the restart. This one lets the background drift first, then puts traditional DA on that same drifted ocean.

One real input → processing → output

Input. Restart restart_2024010118 from abozec expt_35.0, cargo Argo / AVISO / OISST under TSISv2.0/src_gmrf/ocn_obs, and nine GDAC Argo locations on 6 Jan 2024.

Processing. Slurm job 13042189 ran four 1-day HYCOM segments to restart_day04 at dtime 44930.75. Job 13044150 ran xprep then xgmrf then xa2inc on that restart (58 cargo profiles, 9226 binned L3 SLA). Forecast job 13044169 applied incupd.2024_005_18 with incflg=-1 (97 finished incupdate lines).

Output. Hourly surface archives through 12 Jan 18Z. GDAC Argo T RMSE for ops is 0.496 °C at analysis time versus 0.912 °C for null. The same day, nespreso_argo is 1.414 °C and nespreso_tracks is 1.815 °C. Traditional DA beat the free run. Profile-only NeSPReSO did not.

What this explicitly does not do

  • It does not use a pre-built qcobs_tsisv2/tsis_obs_gomb4_2024010600.nc. That directory only has 2–4 Jan. ops is built by xprep from raw ocn_obs.
  • It does not assimilate L3 SST. xprep printed no L3 SST observations. Gates are Profile T, L3 SLA T, L4 SLA T, L3 SST F, L4 SST T.
  • It does not write velocity increments. uvl and vvl are zero in every member increment, including ops.
  • It does not use the 0.25° dense Gulf grid as a member. Tracks are NeSPReSO along SLA geometry, not a full grid.
  • It does not score L4 SST or SSH skill. Eval is GDAC Argo plus twin-minus-null maps.
  • It does not overwrite da_loop_20240102.

Modules

paths.py / paths.sh

Responsibility. Campaign dates, members, machine paths, TSIS and HYCOM binaries, including XPREP and OCN_OBS.

Inputs. None at runtime. Constants only.

Outputs. Imported by every other script. Shell scripts source paths.sh.

Why. MEMBERS and APPLY_MEMBERS are the one place that says ops is a DA twin. The 2 Jan loop used different dtimes.

stage_hycom.sh

Responsibility. Writable run directory, forcing, nest days 001–013, owned hycom binary, restart, optional incup files.

Inputs. Member name. For forecast members, freerun/restarts/restart_day04 and TSIS incupd.2024_005_18.

Outputs. $CAMPAIGN/hycom/$MEMBER/.

Why. Nest files must be three-digit 001…013. A seq -w 1 13 mistake would silently skip them.

forecast_loop.py

Responsibility. One-day HYCOM segments. Sets limits and incflg, launches srun -n 92, rotates restart, moves archives to arch_dayNN.

Inputs. --member --phase freerun|forecast --days --start-day.

Outputs. Daily restarts, hourly archs/archv, log under logs/hycom_$MEMBER.log.

Why. Apply only on forecast day 1 of members in APPLY_MEMBERS. Free run and null stay at incflg=0.

fetch_argo.py / build_obs.py

Responsibility. GDAC sites and 6–12 Jan eval profiles. NeSPReSO T/S packed into TSIS obs/inov NetCDFs with satellites filled to SPVAL and obsgrp profiles-only. Does not build the ops obs file.

Inputs. Drifted archive, GDAC geo files, NeSPReSO APIs, 4 Jan qcobs L3 SSH mask, obs_error_profiles.nc.

Outputs. obs/tsis_obs_gomb4_2024010600_nespreso_*.nc, sanity JSON, site lists.

Why. Innovations must be NeSPReSO minus the drifted HYCOM column, not minus a reanalysis snapshot from four days earlier.

run_tsis.sh

Responsibility. Stage an xgmrf rundir from the drifted restart. For ops, rewrite nlist paths, run xprep, then xgmrf and xa2inc. For NeSPReSO, copy the pre-built masked obs and skip xprep.

Inputs. Member name. Requires restart_day04. NeSPReSO also needs obs NetCDFs. Ops needs OCN_OBS.

Outputs. inc.2024_005_18.nc, incup/incupd.2024_005_18.[ab], copies under obs/ for ops, gate snippets in logs/gates_$MEMBER.txt.

Why. qcobs_tsisv2 has no 6 Jan file. xprep is how traditional DA exists on this date. Nested srun from a login shell has died inside Cooper-Haines. Use the Slurm file in logs/submit_tsis_ops.slurm. Do not rm -rf the rundir if you only need to re-run xgmrf.

eval_loop.py / animate_forecast.py

Responsibility. Score Argo RMSE, increment stats, twin-minus-null, TSIS gates, HYCOM log readiness. Render the 3×7 hourly movie (differences vs ops).

Inputs. Forecast archv/archs, GDAC JSON, increment NetCDFs, ops L3 SSH overlay.

Outputs. eval/REPORT.md, eval/report.json, eval/figures/drift_vs_null.mp4, copies under maps_media/.

Why. Numbers without maps hide the track-geometry increment. Maps without Argo RMSE hide that NeSPReSO hurt the floats while ops helped them.

Workflow 2. Build observations on the drifted state

Entry. NeSPReSO: build_obs.py main(), argument argo, tracks, or both. Ops: run_tsis.sh ops xprep branch.

Transformations. NeSPReSO loads drifted 3D T/S/thk, fetches the profile or grid API, layer-averages onto HYCOM interfaces, masks every satellite obs/inov variable, sets obsgrp[0]=1. Ops rewrites tsis.nlist so ocn_obs and tsis_obs_location point at this rundir, then xprep reads cargo Argo (±4 d), bins L3 SLA, fills L4 SLA and L4 SST.

External calls. https://ozavala.coaps.fsu.edu/nespreso_profile and /nespreso_grid. Ops reads OCN_OBS on disk, no HTTPS.

State. obs/sanity_*.json, casts/*.nc, obs/tsis_obs_gomb4_2024010600_ops.nc.

Output. Argo, 9 profiles, 290 T and 290 S flags. Tracks, 2660 cargo L3 SLA points from 4–5 Jan, 392 sites after 0.25° thinning, 119 kept columns, 3799 T and S flags. Ops xprep, 58 profiles, 9226 binned L3 SLA, no L3 SST.

Failure. build_obs.py exits if the drifted archive is missing, the API returns nothing, or every column is land/empty. run_tsis.sh exits if xprep does not write obs/inov, or if any of the three nlist replace strings is absent from expt_35.0 tsis.nlist.

Workflow 3. Score and animate

Entry. eval_loop.py:main and animate_forecast.py:main.

Transformations. Eval loads each 3D archive once, interpolates HYCOM T/S to Argo pressures, pools RMSE. Twin-minus-null uses layer-1 T and SSH. Animation loads hourly archs SST/SSS/SSH for four members, plots absolute fields and null−ops / Argo−ops / tracks−ops, overlays Argo, ops L3 SLA, and track sites, encodes 6 fps H.264. Hours at or before 5 Jan 18Z come from hycom/freerun and the difference columns are identically zero.

External calls. Static ffmpeg at scratch/tools/ffmpeg-7.0.2-amd64-static/ffmpeg.

State. Read-only on archives. Writes eval/ and eval/frames_forecast/.

Output. REPORT.md, 264-frame movie 2024_001_19 … 2024_012_18 (96 h shared free run, then 24 h apply, then 6 d forecast), stills at 5 Jan 18Z, 6 Jan 00Z, 6 Jan 18Z, 12 Jan 18Z. Anim job 13044452 COMPLETED in 00:00:44.

Failure. Eval records missing if an archive or JSON is absent. Animation exits if the four members do not share a timestamp, or if free-run stamps overlap forecast stamps.

Assumptions, gaps, risks, unverified

  • Assumption. Cargo L3 SLA along-track points from 4 Jan and 5 Jan are the sampling geometry for the tracks member. That is the two calendar days before the 6 Jan 00Z analysis. 6 Jan tracks are not used.
  • Gap. No L4 SST/SSH eval. Report is Argo plus twin-minus-null maps.
  • Gap. 6 Jan Argo RMSE is in-sample for nespreso_argo (those 9 sites) and for ops (cargo ±4 d includes them). +24 h and +48 h are still inside the cargo window. Only +168 h (12 Jan) is outside p_twin_end=4 for ops.
  • Risk. Thickness increment max_abs is ~3.1e6 for ops and ~2.8e6 for NeSPReSO. HYCOM still completed with 0 neg dp on the forecast members. Treat that field as untrusted until someone dumps wet-only stats.
  • Risk. run_tsis.sh starts with rm -rf $RUN_DIR/*. A second ops attempt that only needed xgmrf would delete the xprep obs. The recover path is the existing Slurm file, not a fresh run_tsis.sh ops.
  • Unverified. Native NeSPReSO versus those nine GDAC profiles on 6 Jan was not recomputed in this campaign. The previous 2 Jan loop had ~0.86 °C RMSE and a cold bias. That number is not a 6 Jan measurement.

How to read that

Traditional DA on this drifted ocean works. Ops cut analysis-time Argo T RMSE from 0.91 to 0.50, wrote a real SSH increment (rms 0.075), and was still ahead of null at +168 h (0.75 vs 0.85) after the cargo profile window had closed.

Profile-only NeSPReSO on the same background still loses to doing nothing. The nine-float member is worse at the floats it assimilated, then forgets the increment within a day. Domain layer-1 T rms vs null is 0.076 °C. Look at column 3 versus column 1 in the day-7 still. They are the same picture.

The track member is the opposite problem. 419 columns is enough to matter. T increment rms 0.35 °C versus 0.047 for Argo. The movie's tracks−ops SSH is the tell. TSIS wrote ssh=0 and u/v=0, then layer thickness and T/S still moved steric height along the sampling pattern. Those streaks are the observation operator showing through, not Loop Current dynamics you would want. Independent Argo T RMSE stays ~1.6–1.8 °C for a week.

If you take one sentence away, take this. After four days of drift, cargo Argo plus satellites beat the free run against GDAC Argo. NeSPReSO T/S columns in a profile-only TSIS cycle did not.
